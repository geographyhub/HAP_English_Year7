Your exam

1 hourHow long your exam is.
1 extractA short part of The Breadwinner, the book you study in class.
1 penAll you need to bring.
You will read the extract, then answer a series of comprehension questions about it. These check how well you understand what you have read.

How to prepare

  1. Look back through your exercise book at the tasks and questions you did on The Breadwinner.
  2. Missed a lesson? Make sure you read the parts of the book that you missed.

To get the very top marks

  1. Comment on the writer’s ideas and attitudes: what the writer thinks, and how they feel about it.
  2. Comment on the writer’s methods: the choices the writer makes, like their words, and how these shape the meaning.
  3. Choose quotes from the text to back up your ideas and opinions.
